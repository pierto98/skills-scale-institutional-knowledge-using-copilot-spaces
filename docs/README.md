# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository centralizes the key project management practices used by OctoAcme. The team follows a structured lifecycle that begins with project initiation, moves into planning, and then progresses through execution, release, and continuous improvement. In initiation, the organization validates the business need, defines success measures, aligns stakeholders, and documents the initial milestones, risks, and resource needs in a project one-pager. Once approved, the team turns the initiative into a planned backlog with clear owners, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and a definition of done.

During execution, work is delivered iteratively through a project board, with progress tracked from backlog to in-progress to review, QA, and done. Daily standups, weekly syncs, demos, and escalations keep the team aligned, while metrics and quality gates help track delivery health. The team uses clear role ownership to coordinate Product, Project, engineering, and QA responsibilities, and it manages risks and stakeholder communication through documented status updates and escalation paths.

Before release, teams confirm the work meets acceptance criteria, passes CI and security checks, and has release notes and rollback plans prepared. Deployments are verified in staging and production, with post-deploy checks and operational communication to stakeholders. After shipping, the team closes the project loop with retrospectives that capture what went well, what needs improvement, and which action items should be tracked. This continuous improvement cycle ensures the organization learns from each milestone and adapts its process over time.
